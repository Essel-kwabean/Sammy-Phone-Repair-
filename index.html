// What we fix

From a cracked glass to a dead board — one bench, four specialties.

Every job starts on the diagnostic bench, not with guesswork. We tell you what's actually wrong before we touch a screwdriver.

BEFORE → AFTER
TCKT-01 / SCREEN

Screen Replacement

Cracked glass, dead pixels, ghost touches or a display that's gone dark — we fit and calibrate a true-to-original panel and check touch response across every corner before you leave.

  • Full HD, original-technology panels
  • True-tone & brightness recalibrated
  • Digitizer and Face/Touch ID re-verified
STATUS: BOARD EXPOSED
TCKT-02 / BATTERY & BOARD

Battery & Charging Repair

Fast drain, swelling, or a phone that won't hold charge — we pull the board carefully, swap the cell, and check the charging IC so the fix lasts longer than a week.

  • Capacity & charge-cycle checked on install
  • Charging port and IC diagnostics included
  • Old battery disposed of safely
TEMP: LIVE SOLDER
TCKT-03 / BOARD-LEVEL

Micro-soldering & Liquid Damage

No power, no display, water-damaged — this is the work that separates a repair shop from a screen shop. We trace the fault chip by chip and rework it under magnification.

  • Component-level fault tracing
  • Ultrasonic cleaning for liquid damage
  • Short-circuit & corrosion repair
STATUS: DIAGNOSING
TCKT-04 / DIAGNOSTICS

Free Diagnosis

Not sure what's wrong? Drop it with us. We run a full check — power, board, camera, speakers, sensors — and give you an honest yes-or-no before any money changes hands.

  • Full functional test, 15+ points
  • Written quote before any repair starts
  • No fix, no fee
